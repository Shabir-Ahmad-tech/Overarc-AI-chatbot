FAQ

Questions
we get a lot.

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp — we usually reply within the hour.

Ask on WhatsApp
Do I need a website to use this?
No. You get a standalone chat link you can share on WhatsApp, Instagram bio, Google Business, or anywhere. If you do have a website, we include a one-line embed code.
How long does delivery actually take?
3 business days after you send your form. Day 1: we configure. Day 2: we build and test. Day 3: we deliver with a Loom walkthrough video. Some clients go live on Day 2.
Does it really understand Urdu?
Yes — full Urdu, Roman Urdu, and English. The AI detects whichever language the customer types in and responds in that same language automatically. No extra configuration.
Can I update the bot after delivery?
Yes. Your admin dashboard lets you edit services, prices, and FAQs yourself — no coding. Or message us and we'll update it for you free during your support period.
Is it real AI or just a scripted button bot?
Real AI — GPT-4o-mini via OpenRouter. It understands natural conversation and handles questions it was never explicitly trained on. Not click-and-button menus.
What's the payment structure?
50% upfront to start, 50% when you approve the final version. No hidden fees. The optional $30/month plan covers hosting, updates, and priority support.
Can medium or larger businesses use this?
The current product is best for businesses handling up to ~200 daily conversations. For enterprise or high-volume operations, we build a custom solution on proper backend infrastructure — contact us to discuss.
